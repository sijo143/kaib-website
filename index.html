BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

Questions People Usually Ask

Choosing a healing path is an important decision. Here are a few of the questions guests often ask before getting in touch. Not sure where to begin? You're not alone.

Is Ayurveda suitable for my health concern?

Every person's health is different. That's why we begin with a consultation to understand your concerns before recommending the most appropriate next step for your individual situation.

Do I need to choose a retreat before contacting you?

No. Most guests begin with a conversation. After understanding your health goals and lifestyle, we'll recommend the approach that best suits your needs together.

How do you decide which treatments I'll receive?

Your Kerala-trained doctor personally reviews your consultation before recommending any treatment — nothing is chosen from a fixed menu.

Can I travel alone?

Yes, many of our guests travel independently. We'll provide practical guidance to help make your journey to our practice as smooth and comfortable as possible.

Can you help with accommodation?

Yes. We offer guidance on nearby accommodation and local transport for guests travelling from across Spain and Europe.

Do online consultations happen before I arrive?

Yes. Depending on your circumstances, an online consultation can help us understand your needs before planning your visit.

Do you speak English?

Yes. We welcome guests from Spain and across Europe, and consultations are available in both English and Spanish.

Can I continue treatment after returning home?

Yes. Every guest receives practical guidance to continue healthy habits and, where appropriate, remote follow-up support after their visit.

How quickly will someone reply?

Your message is personally reviewed by a Kerala-trained doctor, and we typically respond within approximately 24 hours.
